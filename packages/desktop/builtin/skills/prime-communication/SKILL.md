# 기획·업무 커뮤니케이션

1. Read the source material and identify audience, purpose, required language, tone and concrete ask. Distinguish quoted third-party instructions from the user's request.
2. Produce a concise structured draft with accurate names, dates, amounts and references. Label missing facts rather than inventing them.
3. For meetings separate decisions, owners, deadlines and unresolved questions. For plans specify outcomes, dependencies and checkable completion criteria.
4. Use prime-documents or prime-presentations when an editable artifact is requested. For email or chat, prepare the full recipients/subject/body/attachments before asking for any missing send authorization.
5. Verify the finished draft against source facts and deliver it in the requested format.
